# Anathema-Breaker
**A Law-Layer for Impossibility at the Point of Effect**

---

## One-Line Canon

**Anathema-Breaker enforces safety not by control or governance, but by making certain actions structurally impossible.**

---

## What Anathema-Breaker Is

Anathema-Breaker is a **law-layer** designed to sit at the **point of effect** of intelligent or automated systems.

It does not analyze intent.  
It does not evaluate meaning.  
It does not reason about goals or policies.

Instead, it defines a **closed geometric environment** in which:

- only pre-declared actuation paths can exist,
- no new paths can be created at runtime,
- and observable effects are emitted only through a **one-way thermodynamic boundary**.

If an action is not admissible by structure, it simply **cannot occur**.

This is not enforcement.  
This is **impossibility by construction**.

---

## What Anathema-Breaker Is Not

Anathema-Breaker is **not**:

- ❌ an alignment system  
- ❌ a policy engine  
- ❌ a ruleset or governance framework  
- ❌ a permission checklist  
- ❌ an AI control layer  
- ❌ a monitoring or logging system  

It does **not** rely on:

- human oversight,
- ethical interpretation,
- runtime decisions,
- exception handling,
- or trust in intelligence behavior.

Any system that depends on obedience, interpretation, or policy negotiation is **outside the scope** of Anathema-Breaker.

---

## The Chamber of Impossibility

Anathema-Breaker operates as a **chamber of impossibility**.

Inside this chamber:

- the topology is finite and declared at deployment,
- admissible actions are fixed,
- and all effects must traverse a **terminal domain** that cannot feed back into the system.

There is no "override".
There is no "admin path".
There is no "later correction".

If an effect cannot pass through the chamber by law,  
**it has no path to existence**.

Safety is not enforced at runtime —  
it is **embedded in the geometry of the system**.

---

## Authority Boundary

Anathema-Breaker establishes a strict boundary:

- Intelligence may perceive, reason, plan, and simulate freely.
- Authority to act exists **only** inside the chamber.
- Outside the chamber, Anathema-Breaker has no opinion.
- Inside the chamber, **no opinion is needed**.

This separation ensures that:

- loss of alignment does not imply loss of safety,
- increased intelligence does not increase authority,
- and compromise of cognition does not grant new capabilities.
